# Pricing — Kadi Research Studio

Kadi uses prepaid token packs, not subscriptions. One token is one model token, charged after the answer arrives. Failed calls are never billed. Every new account starts with 100,000 free tokens — no card required.

## Starter pack
- Price: $10 (one-time)
- Tokens: 250,000
- Best for: roughly fifteen full article reviews, or a term of section drafting

## Research pack
- Price: $25 (one-time)
- Tokens: 1,000,000
- Best for: a working thesis season — reviews, synthesis and full drafts

## Department pack
- Price: $50 (one-time)
- Tokens: 5,000,000
- Best for: a lab or faculty group running several notebooks in parallel

## Notes
- Tokens are consumed by actual model usage and are debited only after a successful call.
- Payments are processed by Paystack.
- Administrators are never gated on a token balance.
